About DeepScriptum

DeepScriptum is an advanced project designed to seamlessly convert various document types (like PDF) into Markdown (.md) format using OCR and AI. It enables real-time collaborative editing, perfect for developers, writers, and teams looking to streamline documentation and content creation.

Powerful OCR & AI

Accurately extract text and structure from your documents, converting them into clean Markdown.

Real-time Collaboration

Work together with your team on the same document simultaneously, seeing changes as they happen.
